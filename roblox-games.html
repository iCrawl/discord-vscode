💡 Roblox Tips & Knowledge
🛡️
Stay Safe Online
Never share your Roblox password or personal info in game chat. Enable 2FA on your account. Report anyone asking for account info.
💰
Smart Robux Spending
Only buy Robux from the official Roblox site. Free Robux generators are scams 100% of the time. Prioritize cosmetics over gameplay advantages.
🎯
Getting Better at FPS
Lower your sensitivity, practice in aim trainers, learn map callouts, and focus on positioning before worrying about raw aim.
👥
Private Servers
Many games offer free or cheap private servers. Great for learning mechanics without pressure, practicing with friends, or just chilling.
⚙️
Performance Settings
Lower graphics quality in competitive games for better FPS. Shift+F5 or the Roblox menu lets you adjust this mid-game without restarting.
📱
Mobile vs PC
FPS and platformers are significantly easier on PC. Horror and roleplay games work great on mobile. Check if a game has mobile controls before jumping in.
🔍
Finding New Games
Use the "Sort by: Rating" filter instead of "Popular". Popular shows what's trending (often brainrot); rated shows what players actually enjoy.
🗺️
Every Game Has a Wiki
Stuck in Deepwoken or Doors? Almost every major Roblox game has a community wiki with guides, entity info, and progression maps. Use them.
